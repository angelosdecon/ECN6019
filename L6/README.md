# Week 6: monopoly workout (self-paced exercise)

Each phone works through a monopoly problem with its **own numbers** (16 sets; demand P = a − bQ, MC = AC = c), one multiple-choice step at a time, with feedback straight after each answer:

1. Marginal revenue (twice the slope)
2. Output where MR = MC
3. Price from the demand curve
4. Profit (P − AC)Q
5. Deadweight loss (triangle between demand and MC, from Q_m to Q_c)
6. Elasticity from the markup, (P − MC)/P = −1/e

Bonus for early finishers: long-run equilibrium (AC, efficient scale, long-run price, number of firms).

The first answer to each step is what counts ("right first time"). Wrong options are tagged with the usual mistake (e.g. "set P = MC", "forgot the ½"), and the results screen shows the most common one at each step.

Dashboard steps: 1 Join (QR) → 2 Exercise open (live % right per step) → 3 Results → 4 End. Phones wait on "You're in" until step 2; after that each student goes at their own pace (they can keep going on the results screen).

About 5 minutes. Parameters and wording: `js/common.js`.
