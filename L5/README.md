# Week 5 game: run your own stall

Each phone runs a street-food stall (bubble tea, bao buns, falafel or churros; assigned at random). Cost of q trays a day: rent + c·q + q², so the extra cost of each tray rises. Every kind of stall breaks even at £12 (minimum AC) but has a different shutdown price (£2, £4, £6, £8).

| Day | Event | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Price £14 | p = MC: add trays while the extra cost ≤ price |
| 2 | Rent doubled, price £14 | Fixed cost does not change output; stay open at a loss |
| 3 | Rainy day, price £6 | Shutdown rule: open only if price covers AVC (tea and bao open, falafel and churros close) |
| 4–5 | Market day: price = 17 − (average trays per stall) | Price-takers together set the price; equilibrium ≈ £13 |
| 6 | Heatwave: price = 25 − (average trays) | Demand shift, move up MC; equilibrium ≈ £18.50 |

Final screen: leaderboard (top 10), class average vs playing p = MC by stall type.

About 8–10 minutes: 35 seconds to choose each day. Skip day 5 (step menu) if short of time.
Rules, prices and timings: top of `js/common.js`.
