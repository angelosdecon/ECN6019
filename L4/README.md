# Week 4 games

**Public goods game** (social preferences): groups of 4, 20 tokens a round, the pot is doubled and shared. Rounds 1–4 without punishment, rounds 5–6 with costly punishment (1 token to remove 3).

**Fake a coin** (biases in judging probabilities): students tap H/T 20 times trying to look random; the dashboard compares their streaks and switches with real coins, then "spot the fake".

Set-up, Firebase and in-class instructions: see the README at the top of the repository. Rules and timings: top of `js/common.js`.
